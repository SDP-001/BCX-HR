My AI Project

This AI assistant is called ByteCodX HR AI (BCX-HRchatbot).

It is developed using Python and FastAPI with an intelligent RAG system and Ollama LLM support.

The purpose of this AI is to answer questions about ByteCodX, HR policies, employee services, recruitment, attendance, leaves, and documents provided by users.
